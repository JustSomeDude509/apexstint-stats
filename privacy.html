Privacy policy

ApexStint stats is a private, single-user tool. Its only user is the owner of the ApexStint YouTube channel.

  • What it accesses: read-only YouTube Analytics and YouTube data for the owner's own channel (scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly).
  • What it stores: channel statistics, kept on the owner's own computer. The sign-in key is stored encrypted on that computer.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • Changes: the tool never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
